Holecraft Send: settings

When a model or a zip comes down on MakerWorld, Printables or Thingiverse, this add-on keeps a copy for a while so that the toolbar button can open it in Holecraft or Trident. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press one of those buttons, and only to the addresses below.

Holecraft

Trident

The key is kept in this browser only. It needs permission to upload models (a key for Holecraft’s Send to Trident does).

What this browser can do

    Downloads the add-on has seen lately

    Only on the three sites, and only ones sent as a download. The part of an address after the “?” is left out. If a download is not offered by the toolbar button, this shows why.
